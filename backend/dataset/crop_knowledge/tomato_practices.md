# Approved Package of Practices for Tomato Cultivation (ICAR / Indian Conditions)

## I. Climatic and Soil Requirements
*   **Climate:** Tomato is a warm-season crop. It thrives in temperatures between 21°C and 24°C. High temperatures (above 32°C) combined with high humidity lead to crop diseases and reduced fruit set.
*   **Soil:** Well-drained, fertile sandy loam or clay loam soils with a pH range of 6.0 to 7.0 are ideal. Avoid waterlogged soils, as tomatoes are highly sensitive to standing water.

## II. Crop Seasons (India)
1.  **Kharif:** Sowing in May–June, transplanting in June–July.
2.  **Rabi:** Sowing in September–October, transplanting in October–November.
3.  **Zaid (Summer):** Sowing in January–February, transplanting in February–March.

## III. Nursery Management
*   **Seed Rate:** 
    *   Regular varieties: 400–500 grams per hectare.
    *   Hybrid varieties: 120–150 grams per hectare.
*   **Sowing:** Raise seedlings on raised beds (1m width, 15cm height) treated with *Trichoderma viride* to prevent damping-off disease. Seedlings are ready for transplanting in 25–30 days (when they reach 10–15 cm height).

## IV. Land Preparation & Transplanting
*   Plough the land 4-5 times to achieve a fine tilth. Incorporate 20-25 tonnes of Farm Yard Manure (FYM) per hectare.
*   **Spacing:**
    *   Regular varieties: 60 cm x 45 cm or 60 cm x 60 cm.
    *   Hybrids: 90 cm x 60 cm or 90 cm x 45 cm.

## V. Nutrient and Water Management
*   **Fertilizers (NPK):** 
    *   Regular: 100:60:60 kg/ha N:P:K. Apply half Nitrogen and full P & K as basal dose, and the remaining Nitrogen in two split doses at 30 and 45 days after transplanting.
    *   Hybrids: 250:250:250 kg/ha N:P:K.
*   **Irrigation:** Drip irrigation is highly recommended. Irrigate immediately after transplanting, followed by intervals of 7-10 days in winter and 5-6 days in summer. Avoid watering fluctuations, which cause fruit cracking.

## VI. Plant Protection (Pests & Diseases)
*   **Major Pests:**
    *   *Fruit Borer (Helicoverpa armigera):* Manage by planting Marigold as a trap crop (1 row of marigold for every 16 rows of tomato) or spraying HaNPV.
    *   *Whiteflies & Aphids:* Vectors of leaf curl virus. Use yellow sticky traps.
*   **Major Diseases:**
    *   *Damping-off:* Fungal nursery disease. Treat seeds with Captan or Thiram.
    *   *Early Blight & Late Blight:* Apply Mancozeb or Copper Oxychloride sprays.
    *   *Tomato Leaf Curl Virus (TLCV):* Non-curable vector-borne disease. Pull out and destroy infected plants immediately.

## VII. Harvesting, Quality, and Post-Harvest
*   **Harvesting Stages:**
    *   *Green Stage:* For long-distance transport.
    *   *Breaker / Pink Stage:* For local markets (1-2 days storage).
    *   *Red Ripe:* For immediate consumption or processing.
*   **Shelf-life:** 
    *   Green mature tomatoes store well at 12°C–15°C for 2-3 weeks.
    *   Ripe tomatoes store at 8°C–10°C for up to 1 week.
*   **Quality standards:** Fruits must be firm, uniform in shape and color, free from blemishes, cracks, sunburn, and insect holes. Grade A requires minimum moisture damage and excellent skin firmness.
